Install on your Mac

Open Terminal (⌘-space, type "Terminal"), paste this line, and press return:

curl -fsSL https://clyon.dev/install | sh

It clones the open source, runs the installer inside it, and opens the setup wizard in your browser. The wizard takes it from there — about ten minutes, two of them yours.

A signed, double-clickable installer is coming; until then, the Terminal line is the way in.
