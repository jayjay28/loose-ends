Support

Loose Ends is open source and self-hosted, and support happens in the open.

Something's broken

Open an issue on GitHub. Say what you expected, what happened, and — if the engine is involved — anything relevant from ~/Library/Logs/loose-ends/. Please don't paste your personal data into an issue.

The app can't reach the engine

  • Same Wi-Fi? At home, the phone finds the engine over your local network.
  • Away from home, both devices need to be on your tailnet (Tailscale signed in on the Mac and the phone).
  • The Mac must be awake — check the engine with curl localhost:8000/health in Terminal.
  • If the app answers every tap with "pair this device", mint a fresh code: lifeline pair on the Mac, or re-scan from the wizard at localhost:8000/setup.

Setup stalled

The wizard at localhost:8000/setup is resumable — reopen it and it picks up where it left off. Every step verifies by doing, so a stuck check usually names the exact grant it's waiting for.
