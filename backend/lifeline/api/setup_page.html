Loose Ends · engine setup

Three things, about ten minutes

Everything here happens on this Mac. One permission, one key, one scan — every wait says what it's watching and moves on alone. Most of the ten minutes is making a Claude account.

1 Permission to read Messages and Mail 1 min · you

macOS guards your messages and your mail behind one switch only you can flip. In the pane we open, press +, then ⌘⇧G and type /bin/zsh, add it, and make sure its toggle is on.

This one grant is the whole reason setup is short: your mail is read from Apple Mail on this Mac, so there is no Google project, no consent screen, and no account with anyone.

2 A Claude API key 5 min · you

The engine reads your life with Claude, billed to your own key. Create one at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys — it's stored on this Mac and nowhere else.

You'll need to add a payment method: the API has no free tier, and a key without credit can't read anything. Loose Ends typically spends well under a dollar a day, and the menu bar icon shows today's total.

3 Pair your phone 30 sec

Open Loose Ends on your phone and scan — or type the code when it asks. Codes live ten minutes and work once.

Don't have the app yet? Get it at clyon.dev/app — then come back here.
